Sector Insight

The clearest sector signal is space/direct-to-device telecommunications. SpaceX’s spectrum acquisition triggered sharp declines in Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile, and ASTS, but The Million Mission argues the market misread the implications: if SpaceX becomes a more direct wireless competitor, incumbent carriers may need satellite-based coverage partners, making ASTS strategically more relevant rather than obsolete. The newsletter also frames broader space names—RKLB, ASTS, and LUNR—as having retraced sharply after the SpaceX IPO, creating a contrarian buying opportunity, although only ASTS received an explicit Buy Alert in the reviewed window. rmb.reuters
